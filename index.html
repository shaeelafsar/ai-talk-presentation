Key AI concepts from the talk

How ChatGPT worksIt predicts the next word, using patterns learned from huge amounts of text.
What a model isA trained AI system — like a brain shaped by examples for a specific job.
How models are trainedThey practice billions of pattern problems, adjust when wrong, and improve over time.
Where knowledge livesNot in a filing cabinet — in billions of numbers called weights.
Different GenAI modelsText, image, vision, audio, music, and code models each learn different patterns.
Why AI can hallucinateIt knows what sounds likely, not always what is true.

prompting guide

Better prompts = better answers

AI is not a mind reader. Your prompt becomes the context the model uses to choose patterns. Specific context narrows the guessing zone and helps reduce hallucinations.

Role

“Act like a patient tutor...”

Context

“I’m in 8th grade learning photosynthesis...”

Task

“Quiz me with 5 questions...”

Format

“Use bullets and simple examples...”

Check

“If unsure, say so. Ask me one follow-up question.”
