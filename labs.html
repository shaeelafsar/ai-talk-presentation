🧪 AI Labs

Lab 1: AI Vision

Make AI see and understand the world around you.

Activity 1

📸 Photo Detective

Take a photo of anything — your desk, a page in a book, food — and ask AI what it sees.

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone (or chat.openai.com)
  2. Tap the camera/photo icon in the message bar
  3. Take a photo of something interesting around you
  4. Ask: "What do you see in this image? Describe it in detail."
  5. Try follow-up questions like "What brand is this?" or "How much does this cost?"
→ Open ChatGPT
💡 Try this: Take a photo of a math problem from your homework. Ask ChatGPT to solve it and explain each step!

Activity 2

🌿 Nature Identifier

Use Google Lens to identify plants, animals, landmarks, or objects around you.

  1. Open Google Lens (in Google app, or search bar on Android)
  2. Point your camera at a plant, insect, building, or anything interesting
  3. See the AI-generated results — species, history, reviews
  4. Try it on food packaging to get nutritional info
→ Open Google Lens
🎨

Lab 2: AI Image Generation

Create images that have never existed before — from just words.

Activity 1

🖌️ Prompt Artist

Describe an image you want to create. The more specific, the better the result!

  1. Open ChatGPT (chat.openai.com)
  2. Type: "Generate an image of..." followed by your description
  3. Be specific! Include style, colors, mood, setting
  4. If you don't like the result, ask it to change specific things

Example prompts to try:

  • "A golden retriever astronaut on the moon, photorealistic"
  • "A cozy coffee shop in Tokyo during rain, anime style"
  • "My school but it's set in a fantasy world with dragons"
  • "A sneaker designed by nature — made of moss, flowers, and vines"
  • "A basketball court floating in the clouds at sunset"
→ Open ChatGPT + DALL·E
💡 Pro tip: Add a style at the end of your prompt like "in the style of Studio Ghibli" or "as a 90s cartoon" or "photorealistic 4K" to change the look!

Activity 2

🎭 AI Profile Pic

Create a unique profile picture or avatar for yourself using AI.

  1. Think about how you want to be represented (animal, superhero, anime character?)
  2. Describe it: "A [animal/character] wearing [outfit] in [setting], [art style]"
  3. Example: "A wolf wearing a hoodie and headphones, cyberpunk neon city, digital art"
  4. Iterate! Ask AI to change colors, style, or pose until it's perfect
→ Create Your Avatar

Lab 3: AI Audio & Voice

Talk to AI, translate in real-time, and create with your voice.

Activity 1

🗣️ Voice Conversation

Have a full voice conversation with AI — like talking to a really smart friend.

  1. Open ChatGPT on your phone
  2. Tap the headphone icon (Voice mode)
  3. Ask it anything! Try: "Explain black holes like I'm 10" or "Quiz me on history"
  4. Interrupt it anytime — it'll adjust
  5. Try different topics: jokes, study help, debate practice
→ Try Voice Mode
💡 Cool idea: Use voice mode to practice a presentation or rehearse for a debate. AI will give you feedback!

Activity 2

🌍 Real-Time Translation

Speak in one language and hear/read it back in another.

  1. Open Google Translate (translate.google.com)
  2. Tap the microphone icon
  3. Speak a sentence in English
  4. Watch it translate to Spanish, French, Japanese — anything!
  5. Tap the speaker icon to hear the translation spoken aloud
→ Open Google Translate

Lab 4: AI Document Reading (OCR)

AI can read handwriting, scan documents, and extract text from images.

Activity 1

✍️ Handwriting Scanner

Write something by hand and see if AI can read it — even messy handwriting!

  1. Write a sentence on paper (try your normal handwriting)
  2. Open Google Lens or ChatGPT
  3. Take a photo of your handwriting
  4. Ask: "What does this say?" or just let it auto-detect the text
  5. Challenge: write something really messy and see if it still works!
→ Open Google Lens
💡 School hack: Take a photo of a whiteboard after class — AI will convert it to clean typed text you can save in your notes!

Activity 2

📋 Instant Digitizer

Turn any printed page, receipt, or poster into editable text.

  1. Find a printed page — textbook, menu, poster, receipt
  2. Take a photo with ChatGPT or Google Lens
  3. Ask ChatGPT: "Type out all the text in this image"
  4. Bonus: Ask it to summarize or translate what it found
→ Open ChatGPT

Lab 5: AI Music Creation

Create original songs with AI — just describe the vibe.

Activity 1

🎤 Your First AI Song

Describe a song and AI will compose it — lyrics, melody, instruments, everything.

  1. Go to suno.com and create a free account
  2. Click "Create" and describe your song
  3. Example: "An upbeat pop song about being done with homework on a Friday"
  4. Wait 30 seconds — AI generates a full song with vocals!
  5. Try different genres: lo-fi, rap, country, jazz, rock

Prompt ideas:

  • "A chill lo-fi beat for studying late at night"
  • "An epic rap battle between a cat and a dog"
  • "A country song about losing your WiFi connection"
  • "A motivational anthem for waking up early"
→ Open Suno
💡 Challenge: Create a theme song for yourself or your friend group. Share it and see who makes the best one!

Lab 6: AI Code Writing

Build websites and games by telling AI what you want — no coding experience needed.

Activity 1

🎮 Build a Mini Game

Tell AI to build you a simple game — and it'll write all the code!

  1. Go to ChatGPT
  2. Type: "Build me a simple [game type] game in HTML that I can play in my browser"
  3. Copy the code it gives you
  4. Paste it into a new file called game.html
  5. Open that file in your browser — your game works!
  6. Ask ChatGPT to modify it: "Add a score counter" or "Make it harder"

Game ideas to try:

  • "Build me a whack-a-mole game with emojis"
  • "Create a typing speed test game"
  • "Make a simple trivia quiz about anime"
  • "Build a reaction time tester"
  • "Create a color-matching memory game"
→ Open ChatGPT
💡 Level up: Once your game works, ask ChatGPT: "Add animations and make it look cool with CSS." Watch it transform!

Activity 2

🌐 Build Your Own Website

Create a personal website about yourself, your hobby, or anything you're into.

  1. Open ChatGPT
  2. Type: "Build me a personal website about [your interest]. Include a hero section, about me, and a gallery. Make it look modern and cool."
  3. Copy the HTML code into a file called index.html
  4. Open it in your browser — that's YOUR website!
  5. Keep asking ChatGPT to add features: "Add a dark mode toggle" or "Add a contact form"
→ Start Building
