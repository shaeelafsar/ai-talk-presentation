# Main Pages and Content Structure

## Homepage
- Hero section with a high-quality image of coffee beans.
- Brief introduction to the website and its offerings.
- Featured products section highlighting top-selling or new coffee beans.
- Navigation menu with links to other main pages.

## About Us
- A brief history of the coffee company.
- Vision and mission statements.
- Team bios with headshots.

## Coffee Beans
- Catalog of different coffee beans with images, descriptions, and pricing.
- Filter options to sort by type (arabica, robusta), origin, roast level (light, medium, dark).

## Brewing Methods
- Instructions and guides for various brewing methods (drip, espresso, pour-over).
- Infographics or videos demonstrating each method.

## Blog
- Articles on coffee-related topics (e.g., bean profiles, brewing tips, industry news).

## Contact Us
- Contact form for inquiries and feedback.
- Address, phone number, email, and social media links.

## Shop Cart
- Display of selected products with prices and total cost.
- Checkout process for purchasing coffee beans.

## Account Management
- User registration and login functionality.
- Order history and account details.
